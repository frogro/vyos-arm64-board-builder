# Internal-WLAN test: management outage, 2026-09-28

All times Europe/Berlin. Read-only investigation after user reported prolonged
remote outage and recovery by Tailscale reboot. No new radio test during audit.

## Confirmed timeline

- 09:56:33 GNOME Network Displays reports STREAMING.
- 09:57:06 ThinkPad kernel: iwlwifi 0000:03:00.0 Microcode SW error,
  ADVANCED_SYSASSERT, firmware 36.c8e8e144.0 (Intel 8265); firmware restarts.
- 09:57:12 NetworkManager removes/recreates ThinkPad WLAN/P2P interfaces.
- 09:57:13 sender reports error. This predates the rollback; previous report
  attributing termination to the timer was incorrect.
- 09:57:29 ROCK rollback runs; hostapd starts at 09:57:31.
- 09:57:35 ThinkPad automatically joins VyOS-AP, making management dependent
  on the radio being tested. Initial homebase check was no longer sufficient.
- 10:11:26 next test stops hostapd; ThinkPad loses VyOS-AP.
- 10:14:28 timer rollback runs; hostapd starts 10:14:29 and ThinkPad reconnects
  at 10:15:01. The first timeout recovery did operate.
- 10:17:18 SSH login from ThinkPad Tailscale address; 10:17:19 manual rollback
  starts, deauthenticates ThinkPad and netlink records wlan0 DOWN. No subsequent
  UP/restarted hostapd is present before reboot. ThinkPad reports SSID missing
  at 10:17:35. This corresponds to a repeated manual rollback over its own
  dependent transport. The expired timer no longer provided independent recovery.
- 11:33:08 ROCK orderly reboot starts, matching user intervention. No panic,
  OOM, hung-task or MediaTek timeout found in the reviewed kernel interval.
  This does not prove the absence of every hardware issue.
- Additional boots end at 11:41:50 and 12:11:00; do not merge evidence across boots.
- After cleanup: hostapd and kiosk active, no receiver running or rollback timer.

## Findings and responsibility

Initial trigger is a logged ThinkPad Wi-Fi firmware crash during concurrent
Miracast/homebase usage. Its precise firmware defect is not identified. Automatic
reconnection selected VyOS-AP. The test harness failed to revalidate the client
route before each destructive radio operation. Tailscale is an overlay and did
not provide an independent physical client link.

Rollback was also unsafe to repeat: it unconditionally resets the radio, uses
systemctl start (not restart) for possibly already-running hostapd, and manual
invocations were tied to SSH. The logs show execution ending after interface
DOWN; process termination due to the lost SSH path is consistent with this,
but the exact terminating signal was not recorded. These harness deficiencies
are the assistant's responsibility, not evidence of a main/release regression.

The container transport-endpoint error observed around later shutdown is not
proven to be the original outage cause. Audio proof and latency remain absent.

## Preconditions for resuming

- Independent wired management on ThinkPad as well as ROCK; avoid relying on
  the same ThinkPad WLAN used for Wi-Fi Direct. Verify actual route each run.
- Boot-ID and route guard immediately before test and recovery; abort on changes.
- All recovery runs as a host systemd service independent of SSH, including
  manual recovery. Keep a timer armed until restoration is verified.
- Serialized, idempotent rollback: skip radio reset when already restored;
  otherwise stop AP first, restore mode/MAC/addresses, restart AP and verify
  interface UP, type AP, SSID and management connectivity. Record completion.
- Do not reuse the current ad hoc launcher as a safe unattended harness.
- Investigate Intel 8265 P2P/STA concurrency separately before reuse as sole
  client uplink. No driver/firmware replacement justified by this audit alone.

## Follow-up authorized by user

User deferred Intel firmware investigation and authorized bounded continuation.
Recovery service and timer added under live-test/; forced AP failure and repeated
recovery passed. A separate ThinkPad timer stops sender/reconnects homebase.
Same-radio management remains a limitation, not independent transport. The next
P2P attempt did not reach streaming; AP/kiosk recovery verified at 13:08:22.
